Orbit Eats Privacy Policy

Basement Games · Last updated 11 November 2026

Orbit Eats is a casual space diner game made by Basement Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The short version: the game works without an account, keeps your progress on your device, and only uses advertising and analytics in the ways described below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (credits, unlocks, upgrades, collected cards, outfits and settings) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the game or clearing its app data removes it.

Gameplay statistics

If you allow it on the first-launch privacy screen (you can change this any time in Settings → Privacy), the game records anonymous gameplay events, such as finishing a tutorial step, unlocking a station, watching an optional ad or reaching a new planet. We use these numbers only to balance the game. They contain no name, email or contact details. In the current version these statistics stay on your device; store versions may send them to an analytics service (such as Google Firebase or GameAnalytics) that processes them on our behalf.

Advertising

Store versions of the game show ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always optional and only play when you choose to watch one. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. Personalised ads are only shown if you agree to them (Settings → Privacy → Personalised ads); otherwise you see non-personalised ads. On iOS you are also asked for tracking permission by the system. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

Purchases (such as Remove Forced Ads or cosmetic packs) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation of which products you own, so the game can unlock them and restore them on a new device.

Children

Orbit Eats is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • Turn personalised ads and gameplay statistics on or off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Delete all local game data by uninstalling the game or clearing its app data.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game uses information, we will update this page and the date above.
